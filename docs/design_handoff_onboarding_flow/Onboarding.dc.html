Novel Mentions · Addendum 05 — First-run / onboarding

Onboarding

Everything a reader sees before the Library. A short welcome (three swipeable beats) sets up what the app is and hands off into the real Library — where the two curated books are clearly framed as ours, and one-time contextual cues teach the rest in place. Built entirely from the existing neutral + accent-ink layer — no new colours, so the kind and status palettes keep meaning what they already mean. Light & dark below, plus the token map for handoff.

Why not a full carousel? A five-slide carousel front-loads every feature before the reader has touched anything real, then dumps them into an empty-feeling app. This keeps the intro to three beats (idea → what you can do → the books you already have), then teaches browsing, notes and logging with lightweight one-time cues on the actual screens. Skippable, and re-openable any time from the Library's ⋯ menu.
Flow 01 · Welcome

Three beats, then the Library

A paged welcome (swipe or tap →). "Skip" on beats 1–2 jumps straight to the Library. Full-bleed neutral canvas, serif titles — the same type and colour system as every other screen, just with room to breathe.
